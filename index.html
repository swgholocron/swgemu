What's changed on Project Holocron

A running record of everything added, tuned, and fixed on the galaxy's newest home base — kept here so you never have to ask "wait, when did that change?"

LOG 2026.09.29 ENTRIES 42 CATEGORIES 9

Travel & Transportation

2 changes

Getting across the galaxy shouldn't be the hard part.

Shuttles and starports board instantly

No more "next shuttle in X minutes." Walk up, board, go — the wait is gone everywhere shuttles and starports operate. Shuttleports still handle local, same-planet routes; starports remain the way between worlds.

Five new worlds within reachwip

Coruscant, Hoth, Kashyyyk, Nal Hutta, and Taanab are open. Find the Holocron Travel Service terminal near Coronet Starport on Corellia to book passage for 5,000 credits from your bank account.

Character Creation

4 changes

More faces, and fewer walls between them and their gear.

16 new playable species

Hutt, Nautolan, Togruta, Chiss, Devaronian, Gran, Ishi Tib, Nightsister, Nikto, Quarren, Singing Mountain Clan, Weequay, Aqualish, Bith, Gotal, and Talz are all selectable at character creation.

Every species can wear and wield everything

Armor, clothing, jewelry, and weapons are no longer restricted by species. If you can find it, you can use it.

More room for alts

Accounts now hold up to 15 characters, with 3 online at the same time.

New characters start with more creditsnew

Fresh characters now begin with 5,000 cash and 10,000 in the bank, up from 100 and 1,000.

Cities & Structures

4 changes

Building and holding a city shouldn't cost more than living in it.

Lower population thresholds for every city rank

Outpost through Metropolis all require fewer citizens to reach and hold rank.

City Hall upkeep cut 50%

Corellia, Naboo, and Tatooine city halls now cost half as much to maintain.

Structure maintenance cut 55%

Houses, factories, and harvesters all draw noticeably less upkeep, galaxy-wide.

Houses hold 10x more

Player housing storage capacity is up to 1,000 items per lot, from 100.

Crafting & Resources

12 changes

Faster benches, bigger batches, deeper stockpiles.

Skill trainer costs down 25%

Every trainer, every skill box — learning from an NPC trainer now costs a quarter less across the board.

Factory production time cut 75%

Mass production runs finish in a fraction of the time they used to.

Crafting and prototyping finish in 1 secondnew

Both "Create Item" and "Practice" now finish almost instantly at the crafting tool, no matter how complex the schematic.

Factory crates hold up to 1,000 units

Batch sizes that used to cap out at 25–100 units per crate now go all the way to 1,000.

Factory crates work mid-combatnew

Pulling items out of a factory crate no longer requires stepping out of combat first.

Resource crates hold up to 1,000,000

Up from 200,000, so a full harvester run fits in a single stack.

Luck now swings crafting rollsnew

Luck and Force Luck skill mods factor into experimentation and assembly rolls from the start, including your shot at an amazing success — not just the lower outcome tiers.

A chance at an extra-amazing successnew

Land an amazing success and there's a 10% chance the quality bonus is doubled.

Some resources spawn perfectnew

1 in 10 newly discovered resources now rolls max stats across every attribute in its class.

Milk yields way upnew

Milking a creature now nets 10x the resources per extraction.

Lair foraging pays off bignew

Digging through a lair for eggs can now turn up 250–2,500 units of a resource, instead of 10–40. Scout and medical foraging are unchanged.

Resource names stopped repeatingnew

Restored the full name pool newly spawned resources draw from, so you'll see real variety again instead of the same handful of names.

Combat & Equipment

4 changes

Fighting together, and keeping your gear in one piece.

Kill XP splits evenlynew

When a group brings something down together, the experience is now divided evenly among everyone who landed a hit, instead of weighted toward whoever dealt the most damage.

Death only costs your buffs in PvPnew

Dying to a creature or NPC no longer strips your buffs. That only happens now if another player lands the kill, or you're carrying a PvP flag.

Repairs got a lot more forgivingnew

Fully broken gear can now be repaired, a repair attempt can no longer fail outright, and every repair now costs half as much of the item's max condition as it used to.

Sliced armor gains full socketsnew

Successfully slicing armor now maxes out its sockets, on top of whatever effectiveness or encumbrance bonus the slice rolls.

Small frictions, removed.

Stability & Fixes

5 fixes

Quiet work, so the loud stuff above just works.

Fixed a startup crash during zone loading

Certain zones could bring the whole server down while loading. That path is now handled gracefully.

Fixed a startup crash from unreachable shuttlesnew

Shuttles that couldn't find a valid destination on their planet could crash the server at boot. They're now cleaned up automatically instead.

Fixed a crash from oversized resource datanew

A malformed resource classification could previously corrupt the save stream and bring the server down. Oversized values are now caught and rejected instead.

Fixed diagonal mission directions

Mission terminals were defaulting diagonal headings (NE/SE/SW/NW) to their nearest cardinal direction. They now send you the right way.

Cleaned up server startup logging

Several harmless-but-noisy warnings during boot have been quieted or resolved outright — no gameplay impact, just a cleaner startup.
